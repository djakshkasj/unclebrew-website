Where it all began
Our Story

A Cup That Started
a Revolution

In 2019, nestled in the heart of Cebu IT Park, Uncle Brew opened its first kiosk with one bold promise: premium specialty coffee at a price every Filipino could afford.

We source our beans exclusively from the Benguet Highlands — single-origin Arabica grown by local farming families at high altitude, delivering that clean, bright flavor you taste in every sip.

Five years later, that one kiosk has grown to 150+ branches from Luzon to Mindanao, with a nationwide turnkey franchise program that's putting Filipino entrepreneurs in business.

"Coffee shouldn't be a luxury. Every Filipino — the student, the driver, the nurse on a night shift — deserves a genuinely good cup. That was our north star at ₱39, and it still is today."

RC
Rico Cabanag
Founder & Head Barista, Uncle Brew PH
