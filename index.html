Our Heritage

From a Small Cebu Stall to a Nationwide Family

In 2020, Uncle Brew was born out of a simple, rebellious belief: Every Filipino deserves specialty-grade espresso without paying ₱200 a cup.

While foreign mega-chains treated coffee as a luxury status symbol, we partnered directly with mountain farmers in Northern Luzon and Bukidnon. By cutting middleman markups and investing in Italian commercial extraction gear, we crafted cups that taste rich, creamy, and authentic.
